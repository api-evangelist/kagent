Lin Sun, Head of Open Source at Solo.io, examines whether Kubernetes Pods remain the right deployment model for AI agents, which are ephemeral and task-based unlike traditional microservices. She introduces "agent-substrate," a control plane abstraction that schedules agents as lightweight logical entities onto shared workers rather than individual Pods.
